Tanaf (Tanaf Kitaun, ceremonial / formal script) — informal redistribution grant
Author: Zavel (Vulcan Quest). Same 2019 zip and grant as Iyik.
Tanaf is NOT Zun-compatible (many more combination glyphs; keys assigned
logically, not as a Zun overlay).
