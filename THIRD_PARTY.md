## hol.kag.org (U+F8FF)

dadap’s README: the mummification/Empire glyph at U+F8FF was copied from
qurgh’s pIqaD font (`https://hol.kag.org/pIqaDFontsNormalAndWeb.zip`).
https://hol.kag.org asks that the page be mentioned if those fonts are used
in a commercial product. This collection mentions that URL for that glyph.
